Same start. Different polytropic paths.

Four gas samples begin at the same pressure and volume and are driven to the same final volume — each along its own polytropic path PVn = const. Watch the final pressure and the work differ even though every piston ends at the same spot. Defaults match Example 2.1: p₁ = 3 bar, V₁ = 0.1 m³, V₂ = 0.2 m³.
