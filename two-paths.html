Piston–cylinder · first law

Same end states. Different journeys.

A gas goes from state 1 to state 2 two different ways. Process A holds volume fixed while pressure changes to p₂, then holds that pressure fixed while volume changes to V₂. Process B follows a single smooth path, pV = const, the whole way. Same start, same finish — but the work and heat transfer along the way are nothing alike.

Try it: change p₁, V₁, U₁, V₂, or U₂ and both processes, the chart, and the results update immediately. p₂ is calculated automatically so that Process B always satisfies pV = const.
